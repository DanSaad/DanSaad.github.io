## GRC

I believe that regardless of advancements in technology, humans will always be a vital factor in securing systems and organizations. Being able to assess risks, educate team members, and ensure compliance with organizational codes and government regulations are essential to the security footing of any enterprise.

All assessments follow **NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1** (Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments) and map to **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)** core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
